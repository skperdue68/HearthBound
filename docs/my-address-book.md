# What belongs in My Address Book?

My Address Book is your personal collection, rather than just a list of things you submitted.

| View or action | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| All saved places | Houses you registered plus places you intentionally saved, grouped without duplicate physical destinations. |
| Your registrations | Houses you submitted or independent house copies you created. The ESO account owner can be someone else. |
| Favorites | Bookmarks to existing houses or tours. Hearts save and unsave them without duplicating records. |
| Tours you created | Your manually created tours, including independent tour copies. Automatic owner tours are excluded. |
| Created & saved tours in Discord | Your manually created tours plus tours you explicitly favorited. |
| Recently viewed | Browsing history, separate from intentionally saved places. Opening a house does not automatically favorite it. |
| Make my own copy | Create a separate editable registration or Private route when a Share Pass permits copying. |

Use a **heart** when you simply want to find a place or tour again. Favoriting does not give editing rights, grant visibility, or preserve access after a Share Pass ends. Removing a favorite removes your bookmark, not the original house or tour.

Use **Make my own house copy** when you need your own nickname, description or tags on an independent registration. The ESO house owner and jump destination stay the same. Chosen visibility, registration policy, duplicate checks and approval rules still apply.

Use **Make my own tour copy** when you want your own editable Private route. Existing accessible house entries may be reused; Private entries are copied where required. Revoking the original Share Pass does not delete your copied route or its new Private registrations. Reused houses still have their own access restrictions and may later become unavailable.

Your collection is personal, but the records inside it retain their chosen Private, Server or Global visibility. All lists still respect the current Discord server, ESO platform/region and access permissions. Older stored visit marks remain intact; this clarification adds no new visit-status controls and changes no existing data.
